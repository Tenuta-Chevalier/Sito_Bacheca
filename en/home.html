Valpolicella Classica

Chevalier

Chevalier is not a fantasy name: it is a piece of France brought to Italy. In the last century, one of the oldest families of winemakers in France came to Valpolicella to acquire an enchanted estate—where today’s great wines are born.

Our story

The Chevalier Estate

Organic since 2009 and then moved to practices with zero environmental impact, driven by decades of experience and knowledge. Located in the heart of Valpolicella Classica between Marano and Negrar. Experience our biodynamic practices and historic vineyards at 350m altitude.
