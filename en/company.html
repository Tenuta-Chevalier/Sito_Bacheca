The Chevalier Estate

Our Roots

Tenuta Chevalier is a unique and artisanal reality

Situated in Valpolicella Classica, in the municipalities of Marano and Negrar, 3 hectares of owned vineyards, initially organic since 2009 but currently oriented towards a zero-impact management system, rooted in the study area of biodynamic and pure ecological cultivation. The vineyard becomes an ecosystem here, within a complex “multifactorial” vision of environmental and ecological respect. The person who made this method possible is Dr. Paolo Zardini, Agronomist and Oenologist, sole owner of Tenuta Chevalier.

"The charm of a very high quality wine lies in the relationship that is created between what is found in the glass, and the person who entrusts themselves to their senses in order to understand it, giving life to an intimate and personal relationship." Paolo Zardini - Sententia Mea

Philosophy

Organic & Biodynamic

Our vineyards at 350 meters altitude with east-west exposure. Clayey-silty, calcareous, volcanic, and alluvial soils.

Organic Since 2009

No chemicals - only Copper and Sulfur using the 1885 formula introduced by Pierre-Marie Alexis Millardet. Guyot and double pergola training systems.

Zero Impact

Moving towards biodynamic and ecological cultivation. Grass-covered vineyards worked seasonally. The future of sustainable winemaking.
