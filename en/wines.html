Wines

From our organic vineyards in Valpolicella Classica

DOC

Valpolicella Classico DOC

Fresh and fragrant, with a dry, approachable profile. Made from late-harvest grapes, it balances light body, lively acidity, and fruity notes.
DOC Superiore

Valpolicella Classico Superiore DOC

From carefully selected grapes, it shows a ruby profile and gently spiced aromatics. On the palate it is harmonious and velvety, with a balanced play of freshness, fruit, and tannin.
DOC Superiore

Valpolicella Ripasso Classico Superiore DOC

Velvety and well-structured, with fruity aromas and a refreshing lift. The sip is full and balanced, featuring elegant tannins and a long finish.
DOCG

Amarone della Valpolicella Classico DOCG

Products

Tenuta Chevalier also produces

Acacia Honey

A small-batch artisanal product; Tenuta Chevalier, with its commitment to the ecosystem, has introduced the quintessential insect—the honeybee—to its lands. Derived from a single extraction, this acacia honey is delicate and fragrant, with a light color and a soft, sweet taste.

Miele Millefiori

A small-batch artisanal product; Tenuta Chevalier, with its commitment to the ecosystem, has introduced the quintessential insect—the honeybee—to its lands. Derived from a single extraction, this wildflower honey is the estate's flagship product and the most complex of the local honeys.

Extra Virgin Olive Oil

An olive oil produced via cold-pressing olives grown exclusively on the Tenuta Chevalier estate. The nose detects a light fruitiness enhanced by vegetal notes of leaf, grass, and artichoke (typical of the Favarol variety); almond notes (characteristic of Leccino); and medium tomato and grass notes (typical of Frantoio). A soft, sweet aftertaste of ripe olive pulp dominates, accompanied by notes of hazelnut, arugula, and radicchio.

Amarone Grappa

A fresh, unaged Amarone grappa that captures the full aromatic profile derived from distilling Amarone pomace. It is fruity and enveloping.

Amarone Grappa (12 months)

An Amarone grappa aged in oak for approximately 12 months. Appare avvolgente e ricca di espressioni aromatiche, calda al gusto ed equilibrata

Amarone Grappa (60 months)

An Amarone grappa aged for at least 60 months in seasoned, toasted oak. Highly aromatic due to the wood's characteristics, featuring distinct toasted nuances. It is majestic and enveloping on the palate, highly balanced, and suited for experienced palates.
