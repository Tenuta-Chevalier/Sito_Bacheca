Wines

From our organic vineyards in Valpolicella Classica

DOCG

Amarone della Valpolicella Classico

Vineyards in Negrar di Valpolicella and Marano di Valpolicella. 350m altitude, east-west exposure. Clayey-silty soils, Guyot and double pergola training. Corvina, Corvinone, Rondinella blend with rigorous grape selection.

AROMA

Primarily fruity scents: cherry, black cherry, currant, blackberry, plum. Dried fruit and in alcohol, jam. Floral scents: rose, violet. With aging, spicy notes: cinnamon, licorice, nutmeg, cloves. The aging in barrels adds vanilla, toasted, coffee, cocoa, chocolate and pipe tobacco notes. The scents evolve and change also in the refinement in the bottle. Tertiary scents arrive: leather, undergrowth, dried mushrooms, balsamic hints of mint and eucalyptus.

PAIRING

Tasty meats, game, roasts, braised meats. Aged cheeses, spicy condiments. Verona classics: horse stew, donkey or duck ragù.
DOC

Valpolicella Classico DOC

Tenuta Chevalier's Valpolicella Classico DOC is a full-bodied red wine, very lean, fresh, fragrant, dry, and pleasant. In recent years, Tenuta Chevalier has adopted the late harvest technique (October) for this wine.

CHARACTER

Full-bodied red wine, very lean, fresh, fragrant, dry, and pleasant.

PAIRING

Excellent aperitif (15-16°C). Complex dishes: fish, shellfish, pad thai, tuna tataki. Hearty dishes: bigoli with duck, pizza bianca, torta fritta, paella, vitello tonnato, chicken curry, pork ribs (18-20°C).
DOC Superiore

Valpolicella Classico Superiore

Coming from selected grapes, the "Superiore" type has a characteristic ruby color and some garnet notes; the slightly ethereal and vanilla scent; the refined, harmonious, dry and velvety flavour. With a ruby red color and an intense and persistent aroma, with fruity notes of cherry and plum, floral notes of violets and slightly spicy notes of black pepper, Valpolicella DOC Classico Superiore Tenuta Chevalier is particularly characterized by a fresh, fruity flavour, with an elegant balance of structure and body and the right harmony between acidity and tannin.

AROMA

Intense and persistent with cherry, plum, violet, and black pepper notes.

PAIRING

Traditional Veronese dishes: lasagna with rabbit, tortelloni with Monte Veronese, cured meats.
IGT Corvina 100%

Rocchetto

It is produced with 100% Corvina grapes from a 30-year-old vineyard that has reached its peak of maturity, but which, when translated into wine, reveals persuasiveness, elegance, and a noble fragrance with age.

AROMA

Ruby with garnet highlights. Port-like, ripe cherry, raspberry, blackberry, undergrowth, dark chocolate, leather, cinnamon, vanilla, licorice, coffee, oriental spices.

PAIRING

Chicken curry, ribs, lasagna, truffle risotto, pulled pork, empanadas, game, spicy barbecue, aged cheeses, meditative tasting.
Recioto IGT

Recioto

A deep golden wine with amber highlights: intense aromas of ripe fruit and noble honey notes, with a smooth, velvety finish. Selected grapes are carefully dried to enhance concentration and sweetness.

AROMA

Ripe fruit, warm spice notes and honey. Velvety, enveloping, long-lasting palate.

PAIRING

Perfect with dry pastries, tarts and fresh Veronese cheeses. Serve 12-14°C.
